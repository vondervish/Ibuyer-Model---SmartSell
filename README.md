Overview
This project analyzes housing market data to support a data-driven iBuyer model. The goal is to evaluate buy–sell strategies, pricing dynamics, and potential returns while considering customer equity and market conditions.

What This Project Does
	•	Analyzes metro-level housing market trends
	•	Simulates the home buying and selling process
	•	Evaluates pricing strategies and ROI scenarios
	•	Explores customer equity retention under different assumptions

Tools Used
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Excel

Note
This project is for educational and analytical purposes only. All assumptions, models, and outputs are illustrative and do not represent financial, legal, or investment advice. Real-world outcomes may vary significantly.
